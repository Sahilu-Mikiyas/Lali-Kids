# Lali brand notes

## Name

**Lali (ላሊ)**, from Lalibela. Legend says a swarm of bees surrounded the newborn
King Lalibela, and his name means "the bees recognise his sovereignty" in Old Agaw.

- Kids say: **Lali**
- Online handle: **Lali Kids** / `lalikids` (plain "Lali" collides with the Argentine
  singer Lali Espósito in search results)
- Brand mascot: **Lali the bee**, who carries stories from book to book. We use the bee,
  not church imagery, so the brand feels welcoming to every Ethiopian family.

To do before launch: Ethiopian trademark search, domains (`lalikids.com`, `lali.et`),
and Telegram, TikTok, Instagram and YouTube handles.

## Characters

| Character | Who they are | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Lali | A small, curious bee | Brand mascot, app guide, rewards |
| Anbessa | A brave, slightly clumsy lion cub | Bedtime and adventure stories |
| Gugu | A gelada from the Simien Mountains who loves counting | Maths world |
| Lulu | A girl who runs a stall at the market | English world |

## Voice

- Warm, playful and short. Sentences a 5-year-old can follow.
- Every story has an ending. No cliffhangers designed to keep kids on the screen.
- English and Amharic are equals. Every story ships in both.
